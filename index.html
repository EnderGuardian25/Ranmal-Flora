Who We Are

Where Science Meets
Living Nature

Ranmal Flora is Sri Lanka's foremost tissue culture laboratory — a place where cutting-edge biotechnology and deep horticultural knowledge converge. We manage the complete journey from cell initiation to field-ready plantlets, delivering genetically uniform, pathogen-free plants to growers across Sri Lanka and beyond.

Our state-of-the-art, environmentally controlled plant factory currently produces 1.2 million plants annually. With a planned five-story expansion, we are scaling to 6 million plants per year — ready to meet the world's growing demand for clean, high-performance planting material.

International Buyers

We Ship
Worldwide

From our laboratory in Sri Lanka to nurseries, farms, and wholesale buyers across the globe — Ranmal Flora handles every step of the international plant export process with precision and care.

01
Grading & Sterile Packing
Plants are carefully graded by size, leaf count, and vigour, then packed in sterile, temperature-controlled insulated boxes — clearly labelled as "Live Plants / Perishables" for customs handlers.
02
Phytosanitary Certification
All export shipments are accompanied by government-issued phytosanitary certificates, ensuring full compliance with destination country import regulations — EU, USDA, ASEAN, and beyond.
03
Air Cargo Delivery
We ship via air cargo with experienced international clearing agents, coordinating end-to-end documentation for a smooth, delay-free delivery to your country.
04
Post-Delivery Technical Support
Our team provides hardening guidance after delivery — including growing media recommendations, misting schedules, fertilising protocols, and pest control during the critical establishment phase.
